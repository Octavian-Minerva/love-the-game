For mates

Look out for your mates.

Most men aren't okay with disrespect. Looking out for your mates is part of the game.

You don't have to be a hero. Small things, on the night and the morning after, make a difference.

Three things you can do

1

Check in the next day

A quiet 'you right?' to a mate the morning after. It doesn't have to be a big conversation.

2

Call out disrespect early

A word about a comment or a joke in the moment, before it becomes something worse.

3

Get advice, not heroics

Never step into a violent situation. Call 000 if someone is in danger, or 1800RESPECT to talk through how to help.

During the game

The morning after

Check in with a mate. It doesn't have to be a big conversation.

Excuses, busted

The game, the result and the drinks are never the reason.

"It was the footy."

A football result does not make a man violent.

"He'd had a few."

Alcohol does not make a man violent.

"He just snapped."

Violence, intimidation and control are choices.

Source: No to Violence (2026), media release, 24 September.
