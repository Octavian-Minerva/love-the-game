Supporting someone

  • Listen, and take what they say seriously, without judging.
  • Let them stay in control. Don't push them into anything they're not ready for.
  • Give them privacy, and encourage them to talk to a support service.
  • You can call 1800RESPECT for advice yourself.

Source: 1800RESPECT

Worried about your own behaviour

If you're worried about how you're treating your partner or family, you can choose to get help now, before things get worse. The Men's Referral Service is free and confidential.

Call Men's Referral Service 1300 766 491

Browsing safely

  • Use a private or incognito window, so this site isn't saved in your history.
  • Quick exit (or the Esc key) takes you to a weather site straight away. It does not erase your history.
  • If someone checks your device, you may need to clear your history afterwards.
