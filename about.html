About

About the campaign

Not Our Game is a campaign to prevent domestic and family violence on State of Origin nights. Its central message is simple: love the game, respect everyone. Rather than suggesting that rugby league or alcohol causes violence, it recognises that violence is complex, and that the game, its broadcasters and its fans can be part of the solution.

The name echoes "Domestic Violence – it's not our game!", a 2007 Queensland Police campaign run with the Normanton Stingers rugby league team (Queensland Government, 2007).

Our approach

Our approach is evidence-based, safety-first and free of blame. We keep the sport and the violence separate, speak to fans as allies, and make support easy to find.

Who made this

This campaign was developed by students at Swinburne University of Technology (ART20003, Skills and Strategies for Social Change).

Not affiliated with the NRL, any club or any broadcaster. This campaign is not a crisis service. In an emergency, call 000.
