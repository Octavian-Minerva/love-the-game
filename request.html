The 2027 State of Origin series

Put family violence support in every Origin broadcast.

We're asking the NRL and its broadcasters to commit to family violence prevention messages and support information in all three 2027 State of Origin broadcasts.

Add your nameGet the toolkit

The request

What we're asking for

  • A family violence prevention message in each of the three 2027 broadcasts
  • Support numbers on screen during the game, including at half-time
  • A QR code to a support page with a quick exit
40.7%

rise in recorded domestic assaults in New South Wales on State of Origin nights

Livingston, 2018, p. 5

The reason

Why the broadcast

Origin nights are a predictable period of heightened risk. The broadcast reaches perpetrators, bystanders and victim-survivors at once, during the hours that matter.

The violence is not caused by the game, the result or the drinking. Violence is a choice, and the broadcast can help people choose differently and find support.

Who is behind it

This request is made alongside specialist domestic violence organisations, with fans backing the change. Every name added shows the NRL that its own supporters want this in the broadcast.
